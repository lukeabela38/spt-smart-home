# Cinema launcher

The TV's only job here is to open six streaming services from across the room.
Keep one horizontal row and the familiar remote controls. The selected app name
is the focal point; decorative movement and promotional content have no role.

Palette: midnight #101d2d, slate #1b2b3e, selected slate #31475f,
white #f4f7fb, secondary blue #b7c5d7, focus white #e7effb.
Use the TV's Samsung/Tizen sans serif: 72px selected title, 38px clock,
31px app labels, 26px instructions at the 1920×1080 design size.

Review: matching text tiles replace inconsistent low-resolution installed icons.
A white outline plus scale identifies selection without relying on colour alone.
Disable the floating tile and costly frosted dock rendering; keep brief input feedback.
Respect reduced motion. Expose listbox selection and live status to assistive tools.
No new runtime dependencies, external fonts, tracking, or background API requests.
